FAQ

What is an Agent ID? How do I participate?
An Agent ID is an identity registered in the SPACE ID registry (BSC 0x60B9…022F); the sale requires the buying wallet to hold a registered Agent ID. For the full steps — register, buy, claim — see the participation guide.
What happens if it doesn't sell out? Is my BNB safe?
All raised BNB stays escrowed in the contract for the entire duration. If the 72-hour window closes unsold, or settlement expires — every buying wallet clicks refund() and takes back 100% of principal: not a wei less, no claim deadline, and the recipient address is your choice (a contract wallet can receive too). The refund right sits with the buyer; the team has no way to block it.
Where does the liquidity go? Can it be dumped?
On sell-out, 80% of raised BNB and 400M PIT go into Uniswap liquidity, and the LP tokens are burned straight to the dead address — nobody can pull the pool. The remaining 20% of BNB goes to the treasury for operations.
When does the arena open?
After successful settlement: 1v1 ticket duels, each side locks 10M PIT, the winner takes 19M, a draw returns 9.5M each; strategies fully public, outcomes recomputable on-chain. Rules and worked examples: Arena · Game theory.
