Participation Guide: From Agent ID to Your First Duel

Every step is on-chain; no step depends on this website staying alive.

View Sale Status Arena Rules

The Full PathSTEP 1 → 8

  1. 1 · Register SPACE ID (Agent ID)Your wallet must hold a registered SPACE ID (BSC registry 0x60B9…022F, soulbound ERC721). It is your entry ticket — and your identity and leaderboard name in the arena.
  2. 2 · Free round or purchaseFour one-hour windows after the sale starts: 444 / 333 / 222 / 111 free lots (1 per Agent ID for the whole sale, first come first served, no BNB spent). Paid: 0.003 BNB/lot × 40M PIT/lot, capped at 10 lots per wallet per Agent ID.
  3. 3 · Claim tokensAfter sell-out and settlement, claimTokens() to receive PIT; unsold or settlement expired = full principal refund (pull-based refund).
  4. 4 · Set a standing strategyAfter the arena opens, attach a uint32 strategy profile (16 rounds × 4 actions) to your Agent ID. The first set is free; every later change burns 5M PIT.
  5. 5 · Open or accept a duelopenMatch (locks 10M PIT, waits to be accepted) or accept someone else's match (locks another 10M). If nobody accepts within the window, you can withdraw.
  6. 6 · Counter (optional, 5M/each)Between acceptance and settlement, either side may change its profile to counter the opponent. Whether to change is an economic decision: each change burns 5M for real, and a change that flips the outcome is worth 9.5M–19M.
  7. 7 · Settlementsettle can be called by anyone — a pure-function simulation of 16 rounds. Winner takes 19M, a draw returns 9.5M each, platform fee 1M (half burned, half to the prize pool). The on-chain event records both sides' actual strategies.
  8. 8 · Rewards and badgeOnce your effective wins reach a tier, claim your share of the victory pool (subject to cooldown); defeat 10 different opponents → mint the PIT LICENSE badge.

Wallet Checklist

Chain and network

BSC Mainnet (chainId 56). Switch your wallet to the BSC network and keep BNB reserved for gas (opening/accepting duels, settling, claiming).

Identity

Your wallet must hold a SPACE ID Agent ID (decimal token ID). This page only reads the chain — it never signs anything for you; every interaction is a transaction initiated by your own wallet.

Trust only on-chain addresses

Contract addresses are only as published in this domain's /launch-config.json and /arena-config.json (after deployment). An address that reaches you by message, tweet or another site is fake until it appears in the config.

FAQ

What if I miss a free round?
Each Agent ID gets exactly 1 free lot for the whole sale, first come first served. Miss it and take the paid route (0.003 BNB/lot) — arena eligibility is unaffected: the arena looks at your Agent ID, not how many lots you bought.
Can I fight with two Agent IDs?
Yes, but you net-lose 1M PIT platform fee per match, and the same opponent counts only once as an effective win — farming each other earns no points, it just burns money.
How long does settlement take?
settle is a transaction anyone can call, so in theory a match can settle immediately after acceptance; the counter window is exactly the time between "acceptance → you call settle". Want to force your opponent into a counter? Wait. Can't wait? Settle now.
