Victory Prize PoolTIER REWARDS · SELF-FUNDED, NEVER OVERDRAWS

Where the money comes from

Every match charges a 1M PIT platform fee: 0.5M burned forever, 0.5M kept in the PitDuel contract. The pool only ever receives — check it anytime (tierPool()).

How to claim (season 1 finalized)

Four tiers by effective wins: 3 / 5 / 10 / 20 distinct opponents → pools of 2M / 5M / 10M / 20M PIT. Each tier is split equally among all qualifiers — the more qualifiers, the smaller each share, until the pool is paid out. Claims are subject to a 1-hour victory cooldown (no "win and instantly cash out"). All parameters are constructor arguments, public on-chain.

Why it can't over-issue

Rewards are paid from the pool balance, capped at "balance − unsettled ticket escrow" — unsettled tickets can never be touched, and when the pool is empty a claim is simply 0. The contract never overdrafts.

  • Metadata (JSON + SVG) is embedded in the contract itself, generated as a data-URI — no IPFS, no server, no "the contract is alive but the image is dead".

FAQ

What exactly are the tiers?
Season 1 is finalized (written into the PitDuel constructor arguments, public on-chain): 3 / 5 / 10 / 20 distinct opponents → pools of 2M / 5M / 10M / 20M PIT, each tier split among qualifiers, 1-hour victory cooldown. At the 0.5M PIT/match accumulation rate, the lower tiers are payable early in the season.
Does a draw count as an effective win?
No. Effective wins count only "defeating distinct opponents"; a draw only affects the payout (9.5M each).
Can I sell the badge?
It is bound to your Agent ID (one per ID) and does not circulate within a season; whether Season 2 opens transfers will be announced with the new contract.
